**El Tesoro de Boturini** - Immerse yourself in a pixelated adventure inside the National Library of Anthropology and History! As Dani, a jammer who during her participation in the Game Jam for the Conservation of Documentary Heritage, gets involved in a side mission she hadn't planned.

### Features

- 🏛️ Levels inspired by pre-Hispanic architecture and codex illustrations
- 🧩 Solve nonogram puzzles to uncover the history of restoration
- 🎨 Pixel art faithful to the visual language of Mesoamerican codices
- 🎵 Soundtrack blending traditional and chiptune sounds
- 📖 Rich cultural storytelling rooted in real Mexican history
