**Dani: Mi Verdad** - Immerse yourself in an Interactive Visual Novel based on classic Mexican soap operas. Experience drama and Día de Muertos themes crafted with the custom 3D Novel Engine.

### Instructions
**Play in full screen!**

- **Movement**: WASD / Arrows (Keyboard) or Left joystick (Gamepad WIP)
- **Interaction**: X key (Keyboard) or Left action button (Gamepad WIP)
- **Camera (Third person)**: Mouse axes or Right joystick (Gamepad WIP)
- **Jump (WIP)**: Space bar (Keyboard) or Down action button (Gamepad WIP)
