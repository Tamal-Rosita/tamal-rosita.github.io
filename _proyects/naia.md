**NAIA** is an atmospheric pixel-art adventure game that takes players on a deeply personal journey through memory, identity, and belonging. Explore handcrafted environments, uncover hidden stories, and piece together the fragments of a world that feels both familiar and otherworldly.

### Features

- 🎮 Classic 2D exploration with modern narrative design
- 🎨 Lovingly hand-crafted pixel art environments
- 🎵 Original chiptune soundtrack
- 📖 A heartfelt story about finding your place in the world
