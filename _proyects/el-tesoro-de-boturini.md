**El Tesoro de Boturini** is a retro platformer that draws deep inspiration from Mexican pre-Hispanic mythology and the legendary Boturini Codex. Navigate through ancient temples, face mythological creatures, and uncover the secrets hidden within one of Mexico's most treasured historical documents.

### Features

- 🏛️ Levels inspired by pre-Hispanic architecture and codex illustrations
- ⚔️ Classic platformer mechanics with a mythological twist
- 🎨 Pixel art faithful to the visual language of Mesoamerican codices
- 🎵 Soundtrack blending traditional and chiptune sounds
- 📖 Rich cultural storytelling rooted in real Mexican history
