About
Us

Who We Are

Tamal Rosita is an indie game studio born from a love of pixel art, retro aesthetics, and stories that matter. We believe games are the ultimate form of interactive storytelling — and we pour heart, culture, and craft into every pixel.

Our Mission

To create memorable gaming experiences that blend Mexican culture, nostalgia, and modern game design. Every project we release is a love letter to the games and stories that shaped us.
